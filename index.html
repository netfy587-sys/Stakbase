Eight remarkable things plants can do

Plants may look still, but they sense, respond and adapt in surprising ways.

Swipe or scroll sideways to see all eight ➜

🌻

Sunflowers follow the sun

Young sunflower buds turn to track the sun across the sky. Mature flower heads usually settle facing east.

🎍

Bamboo grows at record speed

Some bamboo species can grow close to a metre in a single day, making them among the fastest-growing plants on Earth.

🪰

Venus flytraps count touches

The trap closes only after its trigger hairs are touched twice within about twenty seconds, which helps it avoid false alarms.

🌿

Mimosa folds when touched

The "sensitive plant" folds its leaves within moments of a touch, a reaction thought to startle hungry insects.

🌲

Some trees are ancient

Bristlecone pines in North America have lived for nearly five thousand years, older than most historic cities.

🐛

Plants react to chewing sounds

In one study, small plants made more defensive chemicals after hearing recordings of caterpillars munching.

🍌

Bananas are berries

Botanically, a banana counts as a berry, while a strawberry does not. Botany has its own rules.

🍄

Forests may share resources

Fungi link tree roots underground. Scientists still debate how much trees share through these networks.

Plant world records 🏆

Tallest

Coast redwood

The tallest living trees reach over 110 metres, taller than a 30-storey building.

Fastest

Moso bamboo

A giant grass that can add close to a metre of height in a day during peak growth.

Oldest single tree

Bristlecone pine

Individual trees have survived for close to five thousand years in harsh mountain soil.

Patient seed

Narrow-leaved campion

Researchers regrew this small flower from plant tissue preserved in permafrost for around thirty thousand years.

The Stakbase home lab: three tiny experiments 🔬

Simple, safe and fun for kids and adults. Younger children should try these with an adult.

🫘

Bean in a bag

You need: a bean, a damp paper towel, a clear bag.

Place the bean on the towel inside the bag and tape it to a window. Within days you can see the root and shoot appear.

🥬

Colourful celery

You need: celery, a glass of water, food colouring.

Stand a stalk in coloured water overnight. The colour climbs the stalk, showing how water travels up a plant.

🧭

Which way is up?

You need: a sprouting bean in a clear jar.

Turn the jar on its side for a few days. Roots bend down and shoots bend up, guided by gravity.

🔒 Never taste experiment plants, and wash hands afterwards. Keep small items away from very young children.

Myth or fact? 🤔

"Plants need water every day."
Myth. Needs vary. Overwatering harms many plants, so check the soil first.
"Plants only need sun and water."
Myth. They also need nutrients from soil and air, plus the right temperature.
"Plants make oxygen."
Fact. Photosynthesis releases oxygen, though plants also use some when they respire.
"Brown leaves mean a dead plant."
Often myth. A few brown leaves are normal, and roots may still be healthy.
